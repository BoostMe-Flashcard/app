Privacy Policy

Learning data

You can use the app without an account. Study history, favorites and settings are stored on your device. The study features do not send typed answers or study history to an external server. Deleting the app also removes learning data stored on your device.

Versions that show ads

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising. Google may process IP addresses, available device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalized ads.

Depending on your region, you may be offered choices about how ad data is used. If the ad privacy option appears in Settings, you can change your choices there. For details about advertising data, see Google's explanation.
